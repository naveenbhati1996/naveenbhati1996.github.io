About

Solar PV, power electronics and AI

I am an AI Manager in the Department of Electrical Engineering at IIT Gandhinagar, working in the AICoE for Sustainable Cities (VidyutAI) on AI-enabled research and laboratory development in electrical and sustainable energy systems. My current research is on a 10 kW bifacial rooftop PV plant. I completed my Ph.D. at IIT Roorkee on a single question: when part of a solar array is shaded, how do we find the shade, tell it from a fault, re-wire the array around it, and track the best operating point?

Ph.D. research · IIT Roorkee

Four objectives, one chain

Each stage hands a residual problem to the next: detection says shading is present, diagnosis shows it is shade and not a fault, reconfiguration removes the electrical mismatch, and the tracker extracts the global peak of what remains.

Track
Find the global MPP
1

Detect: shading detection

A threshold-free Power Variance Index (PVI) detects partial shading from the electrical signature, and a deep network classifies dark, light and unshaded regions from images to find the shadow boundary. It separates a moving shadow from a uniform irradiance drop.

PVI shading signature3 shade classes93,322 trainable parameters
2

Diagnose: GenRes-Net

A class-wise variational autoencoder rebalances the infrared fault dataset and a lightweight residual CNN classifies 12 fault classes, with Grad-CAM explanations. It is small enough to sit next to a controller or a drone pipeline.

99.00% accuracy, 12 classes4.2 ms per image0.72 M parameters98.4% on RGB without retraining
3

Reconfigure: DOR-SD

Dynamic Optimal Reconfiguration with Shade Dispersion is a deterministic, gradient-guided transposition search that stops in a finite number of steps (at most 15 in the tests). It needs no tuning parameters and no random restarts, so the relay sequence can be proven safe.

+24.3% power vs Gray Code+11.2% vs OECMO≈120× fewer evaluations than GA+141.4% on the 2×3 hardware prototype
4

Track: modified P&O (MP&O)

A two-stage tracker: a directed global scan finds the global maximum, then an adaptive local stage holds the duty cycle when the power and voltage changes are near zero, which removes the steady-state oscillation. Validated in simulation and on a DC microgrid with two energy resources.

98.48% MPPT efficiency< 0.06 s tracking time+15.1% average power vs INC and P&ODC bus held within ±2%

Done · reconfiguration prototype

Relay-switched 2×3 PV array

I designed and built a 6-panel array whose connections are changed by 12 solid-state relays. An ATmega2560 controller reads ACS712 row-current sensors, runs the reconfiguration logic and drives the relays through demultiplexer logic on a custom FR4 PCB. It was tested outdoors under shading sheets, and DOR-SD raised the output power by 141.4% over the unreconfigured array in the test case.

Done · DC microgrid

120 V DC microgrid

I designed and built a low-voltage DC microgrid with a 120 V bus, sizing the inductors and capacitors of the buck and boost converters that connect the PV and battery emulator sources. The modified P&O tracker held the DC bus within ±2%, with control developed on dSPACE DS1104 and validated on OPAL-RT 4512.

Impact

Why it matters

A shadow costs more than the shaded area, and at fleet scale the numbers add up. The figures below are the illustrative estimates from my defence, not audited results.

Case study

7.5 MW farm

A thermal-drone inspection of one 7.5 MW plant estimated about US$ 21,423 a year of revenue loss, of which shading accounts for about US$ 5,627.

Fleet scale

India, 102 GW

Under the assumptions of 10% of energy shaded, an 11.2% power gain and Rs 3/kWh, the recovered energy is about 1.94 TWh a year, roughly Rs 583 crore a year.

Grid

DC and AC side

Faster, ripple-free global-peak tracking gives a steadier DC link and a held DC-bus voltage in microgrids, which means less ramping for the inverter and the grid.

Assumptions: 1,700 kWh/kWp/yr, utility PV cost of Rs 3.5 crore/MWp, tariff Rs 3/kWh. Full details are in the defence slides.
